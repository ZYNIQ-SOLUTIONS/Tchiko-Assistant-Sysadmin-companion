CHARACTER SHEET How Tchiko Lives on Your Desktop

Crafted Like a Pet.
Engineered Like a Sysadmin.

Most AI tools trap you inside a browser tab. Tchiko lives right on your Ubuntu workspace — quietly floating above your windows without cluttering your screen with status tags.

🖱️ Scroll-Wheel Click Input Click your mouse scroll wheel on Tchiko to reveal the compact Ask Tchiko... ↵ bar.
💬 Talking Speech Bubble Press Enter and Tchiko replies in a speech bubble above his head with 1-click 📋 Copy.
🔑 Autonomous Linux Actions Creates OpenSSH keys (ed25519/rsa) for Hetzner, checks ports, and runs bash tools.
🛡️ Antigravity SDK Permissions Master permission switch in GUI Settings & antigravity.yaml for full Ubuntu control.
⚡ 100% Open Source • Ready to Share

Bring Tchiko to Your Ubuntu Desktop

Clone, run the self-test (which also exports all vector SVGs in assets/), and launch.

~/Desktop/Tchiko Assistant
# 1. Clone the Tchiko Assistant repository $ git clone git@github.com:ZYNIQ-SOLUTIONS/Tchiko-Assistant-Sysadmin-companion.git $ cd Tchiko-Assistant-Sysadmin-companion # 2. Verify Cairo SVG export, Antigravity SDK & Ubuntu permissions $ python3 main.py --self-test # 3. Launch the floating Tchiko companion on your desktop $ ./run_tchiko.sh
